Favorite Artists

Tame Impala

Kevin Parker, known widely by the stage name Tame Impala, is a modern rock/pop alternative artist that creates and produces every single one of his songs solo. On tour, he performs with a team of musicians. His recently dropped album, Deadbeat, is going viral online for his song, Dracula.

Taylor Swift

Taylor Swift, the name we all know and love, continues to create music that every generation will love. Beginning her career as a teenager, Taylor began in country and shifted her focus to upbeat pop. She has released 12 original albums, the most recent being Life of a Showgirl in 2025. She also re-recorded 4 albums to reclaim ownership of her music, calling them "Taylor's Version"

Olivia Dean

A newer artist, Olivia Dean is rising in popularity, with her new song "Man I Need". Her new songs come from her second album, the Art of Loving, which earned her a grammy for Best New Artist. She is also the 9th woman in a row to earn this grammy award.

Ariana Grande

Singer, songwriter, and actress Ariana Grande has had many successes in the past decade. Debuting in 2013 with her album, Yours Truly, she has had opportunities in incredibly popular shows and movies such as Victorious, Sam and Cat, and Wicked as well.
